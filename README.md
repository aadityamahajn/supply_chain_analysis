# Supply Chain Analysis

## Data Visualization Observations:

- **Skincare Dominance:** Skincare leads in both sales volume (45%) and revenue (41.8%), with "skincare - Female" showing the highest figures. Kolkata is a key market for skincare sales.
- **Higher Margin Categories:** Haircare and cosmetics demonstrate a higher revenue percentage compared to their sales volume, suggesting potentially better pricing or margins.
- **Logistics Cost & Order Quantity:** Air transport has the highest median cost but also the widest range. Route B shows the highest median order quantity for Air and Rail transport.
- **Supplier Concentration:** Carrier A has the most suppliers, with Bangalore and Mumbai having a significant number. Shipping Carrier B handles the most carriers from these two cities.

## Hypothesis Testing Results:

- **Cost Efficiency vs. Revenue:** No statistically significant linear relationship was found between integrated cost efficiency and average revenue per customer segment (p=0.068).
- **Supply Chain Velocity vs. Sales:** No statistically significant impact of combined supply chain velocity on sales revenue was observed (p=0.671).
- **Defect Rates vs. CLTV:** No significant impact of product defect rates on the customer lifetime value proxy was found based on the statistical test (p=0.765).

## Recommendations:

- **Focus on Skincare:** Prioritize and invest in the skincare category, especially targeting female customers and the Kolkata market.
- **Evaluate Haircare & Cosmetics Margins:** Strategically assess pricing and margin opportunities in Haircare and Cosmetics for better profitability.
- **Further Investigation:** Explore other factors influencing customer revenue and sales beyond cost efficiency and supply chain velocity
